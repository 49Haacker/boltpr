Who We Are

Bolt PR is a public relations and high-quality content production agency with a presence in Mumbai and New York. We work with fashion, lifestyle, and beauty brands that are rooted in local culture and shaped by a global perspective. Our focus is on building visibility through strategy, storytelling, and creative execution that feels intentional and relevant.

We offer customised solutions built on real partnerships. Our work spans editorial placements, celebrity integrations, content production and UGC, each shaped to reflect the brand's voice and goals. With a strong network and deep industry experience, we connect brands with the right audiences through communication that is credible, consistent and aligned with long term vision.

Our Mission

To deliver public relations and content solutions that feel tailored, strategic, and grounded, and we help build visibility through trust, relevance, and intention. We aim to act as long-term partners who understand each brand's path and support their growth with clarity and purpose.

Our Vision

To support brands in growing their presence in ways that are organic, consistent, and rooted in real connection with their audience. We believe that meaningful visibility is built, not bought, and that long-term recognition comes from thoughtful planning and presence.

Why BoltPR

We bring a hands-on approach with grounded industry experience and a strong understanding of what resonates culturally. Our work is relationship-based and outcome-driven with an emphasis on clarity, timing, and alignment. Whether supporting a growing label or a globally managed business, our focus remains the same, which is to deliver a presence that feels authentic and impactful.
