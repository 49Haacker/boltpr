At Bolt PR, we help brands grow their presence in ways that are thoughtful, strategic, and culturally aligned. From establishing credibility in the media to shaping how your brand is seen online and experienced by your audience, we offer a range of services designed to support long-term visibility and relevance. Whether you're building locally or expanding globally, our approach remains rooted in clarity, quality, and creative direction that makes sense.

Services Designed Around Your Brand

What kind of visibility are you building, and is it taking you where you want to go?

Media Relations

We build and maintain strong relationships with editors, journalists, and publications to help brands earn credible long-term press coverage. This includes shaping the brand narrative and positioning to ensure every story feels aligned, relevant, and timely.

Celebrity Gifting and Seeding Programs

We place your products in the hands of high-visibility personalities through thoughtful and well-timed gifting. The focus is on creating organic exposure that feels credible and aligned with your audience.

Brand Activation

We develop brand-first activations that drive engagement, reinforce positioning, and create lasting moments. These often take the form of curated on-ground events supported by digital elements to expand reach.

Campaign Production

We oversee full-scale campaign shoots from concept and casting to production and delivery. These are built to support new collections, product launches, or seasonal storytelling in a way that feels timely and brand-aligned.

Content Production and Storytelling

We create content that reflects the brand's identity and speaks to its audience through consistent, story-driven visuals. Whether it is for digital use or social media, every asset is designed to feel relevant and true to the brand's voice.

Influencer and Creator Collaborations

We manage creator campaigns that feel organic, on-brand, and platform-appropriate. From outreach to execution, every collaboration is designed to reflect the brand's tone and build genuine engagement.

Soft PR and Editorial Placements

We secure subtle brand mentions, feature opportunities, and editorial inclusions that contribute to long-term visibility. This approach focuses on presence that feels earned rather than promoted.

Global Production Shoots

We plan and manage shoots in international locations that align with the brand's mood and aspirations. From sourcing locations and talent to final execution, we deliver high-quality content that supports global positioning.

Social Media Management

We manage a brand's voice across social platforms with consistent messaging and a clear posting schedule. We spark genuine audience interactions that build loyalty and drive measurable engagement.

Strategic Alliances and Partnerships

We help brands identify and build collaborations that extend relevance and cultural resonance. These include brand-to-brand or brand-to-personality alignments that feel intentional and mutually impactful.

UGC by BoltPR

User generated content is no longer optional for brands aiming to stay relevant. At Bolt PR, we have built a dedicated vertical for UGC that blends strategy with authenticity. We collaborate with a curated pool of creators and everyday users to develop content that feels relatable, trusted, and aligned with your brand voice. From product highlights to natural reviews, we focus on content that drives real attention and performance across digital platforms.

Our approach is full circle. We manage everything from sourcing creators and briefing them to overseeing shoots and final delivery. Each piece of content is tailored for platform, audience, and objective, ensuring it lands where it matters most. UGC done right does not just support campaigns, it becomes a part of how people experience the brand.
